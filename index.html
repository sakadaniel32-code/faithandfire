✦ About

What Is
Faith & Fire

Faith & Fire is the annual crusade of The Watchmen Prayer Network International: three evenings of the Word, worship, and prayer on one ground in Abraka. Formerly known as DELSU Prays, December 2026 is its first edition under the new name. Same altar. Deeper fire.

01

Register Your Seat

A one-minute form: your name, your phone, and whether you want in on the WhatsApp group.

02

Get the Direction

Crusade Ground, behind Ese-G Hostel, off Olori 19 road, Abraka. Doors open from 4:00pm.

03

Come Hungry

Bring your Bible, your expectations, and someone who needs the fire too.

04

Stand in the Fire

Three evenings of worship, the Word, and prayer. Leave carrying what you came for.
